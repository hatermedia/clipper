## Publishing order

1. Publish the Fiverr gig or Gumroad product (requires your account).
2. Run the 10 personalized outreach contacts, one at a time.
3. Deliver the first order using `DELIVERY_CHECKLIST.md`.
4. Ask for a testimonial and a before/after quote.
5. Use that proof on the site and in the next 10 contacts.

## Honesty rules

- Say "processed on one local machine, not an AI SaaS" — not "no uploads" for
  the service, since the buyer sends footage.
- Label synthetic demo footage as synthetic.
- Do not promise views, monetization, or platform eligibility.
- Do not take an order you cannot deliver in the stated window.
- Delete footage 7 days after delivery.

## If a buyer asks for vertical/captions

Not in the pilot. Offer a referral or say it is on the roadmap. Do not accept work
the tool cannot deliver.
