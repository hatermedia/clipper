## Long description

```text
You have a folder of gameplay and no interest in scrubbing it.

The Clipper Highlight Pack is a done-for-you first pass:

- 10 trimmed 16:9 highlight clips
- Lossless stream copy where compatible, or clean H.264 MP4
- A clips.csv with start/end timestamps and confidence scores
- 10 preview frames for quick review
- One revision round

HOW IT WORKS

1. Buy the pack and answer the intake questions.
2. Send a folder link through the private intake method.
3. I run a local FFmpeg pipeline (audio spikes + motion), review every proposed
   cut, and export the clips.
4. You download the folder, the clip list, and the previews.
5. If a cut misses, send the timestamp within the revision window.

WHAT IT IS GOOD FOR

- ShadowPlay, OBS, Steam, or capture-card folders
- Twitch/YouTube VODs you own
- Shooters, racing, sports, horror, co-op
- Creators who want a first pass without learning FFmpeg

WHAT IT IS NOT

- Not a vertical/caption editor
- Not a thumbnail/title/publishing service
- Not a semantic AI editor: it finds loud + fast-moving, and I review the list
- Not a license for music or footage you do not own

RIGHTS + PRIVACY

You confirm you own or have permission to use the footage. You keep the rights
to the footage and delivered clips. Footage is processed on one local machine
and deleted 7 days after delivery unless we agree otherwise in writing. Do not
send confidential, personal, or unlawful material.

TURNAROUND

48 hours after footage access and scope confirmation. Larger packs have longer
turnaround; see the package details.
```

## Fulfillment

- Deliver a single folder link containing `clips/`, `previews/`, `clips.csv`,
  and `delivery-notes.txt`.
- Send the delivery message from `DELIVERY_CHECKLIST.md`.
- Delete the footage after 7 days and confirm deletion.
