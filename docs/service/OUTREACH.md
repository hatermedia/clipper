# Outreach templates

Use these one at a time. Do not mass-DM, scrape private emails, or post the same
message in ten communities. Disclose that you are the tool author and that the
paid pack is optional.

## Who to contact

- Small streamers with long VODs and inconsistent clip output.
- No-commentary / long-play YouTube channels.
- Editors who already cut gameplay but want a faster first pass.
- Discord communities with a `#self-promo` or `#tools` channel (ask a mod first).
- Indie studios with devlog footage and no editor.

Do not contact large agencies or channels that already have editors. Do not
contact someone whose footage you cannot verify they own.

## Template 1 — Streamer, 3-clip free sample

**Subject:** 3 free clips from your <game> VOD?

```text
Hey <name>,

I build Clipper, a local-first gameplay highlight trimmer
(https://github.com/hatermedia/clipper). I'm testing a done-for-you pack for
small streamers.

If you send one VOD you own, I'll cut 3 clips for free and send them back with
timestamps. No signup, no watermark, no obligation. If they're useful, the paid
pack is 10 clips for $29 intro.

Want me to do the 3-clip sample? If not, no worries — I won't follow up again.

— <name>
```

## Follow-up (one only, 5–7 days later)

```text
Hey <name>,

Just closing the loop on the 3-clip sample. If you're not interested, reply "no"
and I'll remove you from my list. If you are, send one VOD you own and I'll cut
three clips this week.

— <name>
```

## Rules

- One follow-up maximum. No "just bumping" loops.
- Include a clear opt-out ("reply no and I'll remove you").
- Do not claim results, views, or revenue.
- Do not use fake urgency or fake scarcity.
- Do not send from a purchased email list.
- Do not put private footage links in public issues.
- If someone asks you to stop, stop and log it.
