Please do not send private links or personal data before scope is confirmed.

## FAQ

**What counts as a highlight?**
Loud reactions and sudden motion: gunfights, explosions, camera flicks, kill-feed
flashes, hype moments. I review the detector's shortlist before exporting.

**What if it misses the best moment?**
Send the timestamp within the revision window and I will re-cut it. The detector
is a first pass, not a semantic editor.

**Do you keep my footage?**
No. It is processed on one local machine and deleted 7 days after delivery unless
we agree otherwise in writing.

**Can you make vertical clips or captions?**
Not in this pilot. The packages are 16:9 only. Ask before ordering if that is a
hard requirement.

**Can you use copyrighted music?**
You are responsible for the rights to the footage and any music in it. I do not
license music.

**Refunds?**
If I deliver no usable clips, full refund. If delivery has started, the completed
portion is chargeable. See `SERVICE.md`.
