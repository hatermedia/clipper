# Done-for-you clips — terms & process

This is a pilot service around Clipper, the open-source highlight trimmer. It is
separate from the free software. The software stays MIT licensed and free.

| Package | Price | Clips | Footage | Turnaround | Revisions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Warm-up | $29 intro / $49 standard | 10 | up to 2 hours | 48 hours | 1 |

| Grind | $249 | 60 | up to 20 hours | 5 days | 2 |

The first five orders are $29. Pilot pricing. Prices and scope can change before
a booking is confirmed.

## Process

1. Open a **Clip request** issue using the repository issue template.
2. Do **not** post private footage links or personal data in the public issue.
3. If the slot and scope fit, you receive a private intake method and a quote.
4. Work starts only after written scope confirmation and payment terms are agreed.
5. You receive a download link with the trimmed clips and a short delivery note.
6. Revision requests are limited to the package's revision count and must stay
   within the original scope.

## What you get

- Trimmed highlight clips from footage you own or are authorized to use.
- Default output: source container, stream-copy (lossless) where compatible.
- Re-encoded MP4 available when exact edges or container compatibility matter.
- A simple list of clip timestamps and confidence notes.
- No watermark.

Vertical crops, captions, thumbnails, and publishing are **not** included in the
pilot packages unless explicitly agreed in writing.

## Rights and responsibilities

- You confirm you own or have permission to use the footage.
- You keep ownership of your footage and the delivered clips.
- You are responsible for music, voice, and other third-party content in the
  footage.
- The service does not publish anything to Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, or anywhere
  else.
- Do not send confidential, personal, or unlawful material.
- Footage is processed on one local machine, not uploaded to an AI SaaS, and is
  deleted 7 days after delivery unless a longer retention period is agreed in
  writing.

## Payment and refunds

- Payment method and currency are agreed before work starts.
- No payment is collected on the website.
- Because editing work is custom, refunds are handled case by case. If delivery
  has not started, a cancellation is normally refundable minus any payment
  processing fees. If delivery has started, the completed portion is chargeable.
- Chargebacks or non-payment pause delivery.

## Limits

- Turnaround starts when footage access and payment (or agreed terms) are complete.
- Very large files, unusual codecs, or damaged footage may need a revised quote.
- The service is not a guarantee of views, monetization, or platform eligibility.
